RecompAI is an AI-powered body recomposition coaching platform built on Cloudflare Workers. Track meals via text, photo, or voice with AI-powered macro estimation using Llama 3.2 Vision. Manage supplement and medication protocols with dose escalation tracking. Receive personalized AI coaching through natural conversation and weekly automated email summaries with trends and insights. Includes semantic search over meal databases and chat history, with a native iOS app via Capacitor.
